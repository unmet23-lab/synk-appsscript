THE MASCOT FRIENDS (they must look exactly like the attached reference photos; they are real handmade needle-felted wool plush toys photographed in a real miniature set, not cartoons, not 3D renders):
- Kkamong: a small dark-brown needle-felted wool creature with a short, dense felted fuzz (not long hair) and a soft two-part body - a round head sitting on a rounder belly - two pointed cat-like ears and big round glossy green bead eyes. It has NO arms: at its shoulders are two small dragon wings, each a thin brown felt wing frame with little round elbow knobs and olive matcha-green felt membranes between the ribs. Behind it a short, thick brown felt dragon tail curls up with five small olive-green felt spikes along its top. No hands, no paws, no mouth, no nose. When Kkamong holds, carries or touches something it uses the tips of its wings like hands.
- Mongle: a coral-salmon felted wool dome shaped like a little ghost, with a wavy scalloped bottom hem edged with a cream running stitch. Its face is only two eyes (black glass beads or stitched dark-brown thread, depending on the expression). No mouth, no arms, no legs. Mongle moves things by telekinesis: an object it uses floats a little in front of it.
- Marin: a small figure with a large round navy-blue felt helmet-head with two big round butter-yellow felt lens eyes, a cream knitted body with two small real arms and hands and stubby legs, a small cream cross-body pouch and a tiny white daisy. Marin holds things with its two hands.
