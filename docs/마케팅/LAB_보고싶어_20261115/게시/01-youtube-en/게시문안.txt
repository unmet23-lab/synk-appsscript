제목: Your Korean friend texts “보고 싶어”… don’t send selfies 😳 #learnkorean

설명:
보고 싶어 (bo-go si-peo) = I miss you 💗
Word by word it’s 보다 (to see) + -고 싶다 (want to) — “I want to see you” — but in a text it means “I miss you.”

How to reply:
나도 보고 싶어 (na-do bo-go si-peo) = I miss you too
More polite: 보고 싶어요 (bo-go si-peo-yo)
