# Design

Concept: '네!'라고 말하는 자신감에서 '다시 말해 주세요'라고 물어보는 자신감으로 이동하는 작은 카페 코미디.

- Canvas 1080×1920. Frame rate 24. Safe text area: x 76–936, y 180–1540.
- Preserve photoreal cafe footage; no grade, skin smoothing, or decorative photographic effects.
- Base colors: Paper #FFFFFF and Black #000000. Emphasis: a restrained LAB coral (#D76556); use white-on-black for small text.
- Typography: local SUIT Variable, weights 550–850. Dialog 58–64px, key phrase 84–100px, supporting text 40–48px. No English slogan.
- Approved LAB logo asset, unchanged, with enough visible size and clear space.
- The actors' faces are the focal point in dialogue shots. Captions sit below faces, inside platform safe zones; no large panel over the eyes.
- Practice cards have one phrase or one question. The visible count and progress fill indicate how much time remains to speak.
- Motion: restrained smooth scale entrances from spring-pop-entrance, and a shrinking progress fill from stat-bars-and-fills. Native actor motion and motivated cuts carry the dialogue shots.
- Audio: native generated dialogue and cafe room tone. No new narrator or music to mask Korean pronunciation.
