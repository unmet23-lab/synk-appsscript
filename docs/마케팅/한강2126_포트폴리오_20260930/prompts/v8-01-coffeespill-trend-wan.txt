SCENARIO: overcast afternoon just after rain, a wet riverside promenade in Hangang Park, Seoul. He walks toward the camera with a plain matte-black VR headset over his eyes, playing a racing game. RIGHT hand: exactly ONE generic unbranded clear takeaway cup of iced americano with a lightweight flat plastic lid. LEFT hand: one phone. Black backpack; the plush hangs from its right side on a short chain.
0.0-1.6s SLIP, real time. Low wide angle at pavement level. He leans hard to his right as if cornering in the game; his white sneaker slides on the wet stone and he tips backward off balance in a light comedic slip, arms flying up. The jolt bumps the headset up onto his forehead: face uncovered, restrained surprise, eyes wide, lips parted.
COFFEE RELEASE: his grip opens and the cup leaves his RIGHT hand with one short outward, slightly upward rotational impulse. It travels only 20 to 35 cm. The cup tilts and rotates. Because of the rotation and the liquid's inertia ONE edge of the lid partially lifts; the opposite side stays attached; a REAL gap forms. ONLY AFTER the gap exists does coffee begin escaping: one restrained dark ribbon, a small meniscus, a few connected odd-sized droplets. RIGHT HAND = EMPTY from that instant, fingers spread. The same jolt swings the plush outward on its chain; it stays attached.
1.6-2.4s ENTER BULLET TIME. Smoothly slow the whole world to about 2 to 3 percent of normal speed. Not absolute zero: residual movement stays in the cup's rotation, the coffee and the plush. The camera begins a controlled three-dimensional arc. Establish the complete suspended accident in a low wide tableau: he hangs tipped backward; FACE uncovered; COFFEE at shoulder height on his right; PLUSH behind his right shoulder at the end of its taut chain.
2.4-3.9s FACE. The camera physically travels to his face: head and shoulders first, then closer at 45 degrees to his right. Then it pulls back out.
3.9-6.6s COFFEE INSPECTION. The camera physically travels to the SAME cup. THE CUP DOES NOT APPROACH THE CAMERA. First show the complete cup: its tilt, its slow rotation, the attached lid, the one lifted lid edge, the real gap, the escaping coffee. Then push in to macro: thin clear plastic, the wet rim, lid deformation, the coffee meniscus, surface tension, small bubbles, ice cubes inside, connected droplets. Coffee exits ONLY through the gap. Then the camera pulls back out; cup, lid and liquid stay anchored.
6.6-9.2s PLUSH INSPECTION. The camera travels around his right shoulder to the plush. First the whole plush on its chain against the black backpack, then push in to macro: soft fur fibres, glossy green eyes, the coral tab. In macro the plush blinks both eyes once, quickly: the only magic. Then the camera pulls back out.
9.2-10.0s RECONSTRUCT THE TABLEAU. The camera pulls back to the same low wide framing as at 2.0s: the man tipped backward, empty right hand, the cup with its lifted lid and thin coffee ribbon, the plush, the white felt building far behind. No reset.

11.5-15.0s FINAL. The camera sinks to ground level beside him, 45 degrees to his right. Lying on his back, he slowly lifts the headset off his forehead with one hand and looks up at the sky, calm and deadpan, face fully visible.
