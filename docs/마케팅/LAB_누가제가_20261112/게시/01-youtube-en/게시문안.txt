설명:
누가 = who?
When the question is "who?", answer with 제가 — "I'm the one."

뭐 먹었어요? (What did you eat?)
→ 저는 피자 먹었어요. ✓ (As for me, I ate pizza.)

누가 피자 먹었어요? (Who ate the pizza?)
→ 제가 피자 먹었어요. ✓ (It was me who ate it.)

저는 = 저 + 는: talks about me (the topic).
제가 = 저 + 가: points at the one who did it (the answer to "who?").
