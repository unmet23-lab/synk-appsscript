Creepy Korean fact 🫘 When Koreans get really scared, they say their liver shrinks to the size of a bean!

간이 콩알만 해지다 — not “my liver shrinks to a bean”: super scared
간 떨어지다 — not “my liver falls out”: totally startled
간이 크다 — not “a big liver”: fearless

Say it: 간이 콩알만 해졌어요 · 간 떨어질 뻔했어요 · 간이 크네요
