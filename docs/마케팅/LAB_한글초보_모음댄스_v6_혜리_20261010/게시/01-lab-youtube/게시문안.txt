Sing and move along with Mongle — one song, all 10 basic Korean vowels (Hangul).

Each vowel's short stroke points the way your arms go:
아 (a) → right · 어 (eo) → left · 오 (o) → up · 우 (u) → down
으 (eu) → stretch out wide · 이 (i) → stand straight
Two short strokes = do it twice: 야 (ya) · 여 (yeo) · 요 (yo) · 유 (yu)

Romanization appears under every letter, and English captions (CC) have the lyrics.

At the end, try reading 아이 · 오이 · 우유 before you hear them!
