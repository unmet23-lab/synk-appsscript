제목: Rina and Lina are the SAME name in Korean 😅 #learnkorean

설명:
In Hangul, R and L are one letter: ㄹ.
So Rina and Lina are both 리나 (ri-na) — and Ryan and Lion are both 라이언 (ra-i-eon) 🦁

More names:
Laura → 로라 (ro-ra)
Ryan → 라이언 (ra-i-eon)

ㄹ sounds like a light tap, somewhere between r and l. Korean doesn't tell R and L apart, so one cup can have two owners ☕
