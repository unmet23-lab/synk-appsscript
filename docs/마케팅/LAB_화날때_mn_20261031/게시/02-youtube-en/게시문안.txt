제목: 4 Korean words for when you're ANGRY 😤 …just not in front of your boss #learnkorean

설명:
Bad day? Here's how Koreans say it:

짜증 나 (jja-jeung na) = So annoying!
열받아 (yeol-ba-da) = I'm boiling! (literally "I'm getting heated")
어이없어 (eo-i-eop-sseo) = Unbelievable… I'm speechless.
억울해 (eo-gul-hae) = That's so unfair! (when you get blamed for something you didn't do)

All four are casual. Use them with friends. In front of elders or your boss, smile, say 네, 사장님~ (ne, sa-jang-nim = Yes, boss~) and keep it in your head 😅
