```
We made a 60-second brand film for a heavy-industry company that doesn't exist.

Every person in it, from the welders to the engineer who watches the turbine leave, is AI-generated. Nothing was shot on set.

What we did differently:
– Every shot was drawn as a still first, 34 of them, so the faces, the light and the story were fixed before anything moved.

It's a spec film. If you make something heavy, precise or hard to film, this is how we'd tell its story.
```
