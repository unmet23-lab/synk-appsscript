제목: Sleep "comes" in Korean 😴 잠이 와요 #learnkorean

설명:
잠이 와요 (jami wayo) = I'm sleepy — literally "sleep comes."

Korean uses one verb, 와요 (wayo, "comes"), for all of these:
잠이 와요 — I'm sleepy
친구가 와요 — my friend is coming
비가 와요 — it's raining
눈이 와요 — it's snowing
전화가 와요 — I'm getting a call

가 or 이? Same job: 가 after a vowel (친구가, 비가), 이 after a consonant (잠이, 눈이).
