제목: 서비스 in Korea = FREE 🥟 (not "service") #learnkorean

설명:
서비스 (seo-bi-seu) = FREE — it's on the house 🥟
In a Korean restaurant, when the owner sets down something you didn't order and says 서비스~!, you don't pay for it.
Mongle didn't know… and pushed its wallet over 😅

Words in this video:
서비스 (seo-bi-seu) = something extra for free, from the shop
얼마예요? (eol-ma-ye-yo?) = How much is it?
아니 아니~ (a-ni a-ni~) = No, no~
서비스예요~ (seo-bi-seu-ye-yo~) = It's on the house~

Tip: 서비스 is the owner's gift — it's not something you ask for. Just say 감사합니다! (gam-sa-ham-ni-da = Thank you!)
