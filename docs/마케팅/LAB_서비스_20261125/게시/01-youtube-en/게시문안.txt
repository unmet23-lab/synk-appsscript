제목: 서비스 in Korea = FREE 🥟 (just don't ask for more 😅) #learnkorean

설명:
서비스 (seo-bi-seu) = FREE — it's on the house 🥟
In a Korean restaurant, when the owner brings food you didn't order and says 서비스~!, you don't pay for it.
Mongle didn't know… so the more food came, the more money it pushed over 😅

Words in this video:
서비스 (seo-bi-seu) = something extra for free, from the shop
얼마예요? (eol-ma-ye-yo?) = How much is it?
또 (tto) = more, again
이것도 (i-geot-do) = this too
공짜 (gong-jja) = free
더 주세요 (deo ju-se-yo) = Please give me more
계산서 (gye-san-seo) = the bill
아니에요? (a-ni-e-yo?) = It isn't…?

Tip: 서비스 is the owner's gift — it's not something you ask for. Ask for more and you get the bill 😂 Just say 감사합니다! (gam-sa-ham-ni-da = Thank you!)
