SETTING: The open paved plaza in front of a hawker centre in Singapore. Under its high corrugated roof, lunch diners fill the round tables, with a flat white pocket pack of tissues on almost every free table. Out on the open paving, one branch of a rain tree at the plaza's edge casts the only shade: a narrow patch just wide enough for one person and a knee-high, closed-top black box where a living statue performs. Across the plaza, in the roof's shade by a pillar, stands a mint-green sampling stand with two white handheld fans lying flat on its counter, one folding stool at its end nearer the box and a small tour board at its far end. The plaza's open side, facing the roof, ends in a low hedge and pale apartment blocks.
