제목: I said "SET" in Korea… and got 3 burgers 🍔🍔🍔 #learnkorean

설명:
세트 (se-teu) = combo meal 🍔🍟🥤
셋 (set) = three — so if you say "burger SET!" in Korea, you might get THREE burgers 😂

Words in this video:
버거 주세요 (beo-geo ju-se-yo) = a burger, please
콜라 주세요 (kol-la ju-se-yo) = a cola, please
아이스크림 (a-i-seu-keu-rim) = ice cream
네 (ne) = yes / sure
세트 주세요 (se-teu ju-se-yo) = a combo, please

Tip: English words in Korean get Korean sounds — burger → 버거 (beo-geo), cola → 콜라 (kol-la).
