제목: "Very" in Korean? I asked for ONE word… 😵 아주 · 정말 · 진짜 · 완전 #learnkorean

설명:
English has one "very." Korean has a whole pile.

The 4 you'll hear the most:
아주 (a-ju) — very
정말 (jeong-mal) — truly, really
진짜 (jin-jja) — really (casual, you'll hear it all the time)
완전 (wan-jeon) — totally (slang, with friends)

…and 8 more:
매우 (mae-u) — very (more written / formal)
너무 (neo-mu) — so (started as "too much," now used for "very" all the time)
엄청 (eom-cheong) — super
되게 (doe-ge) — really (casual)
무척 (mu-cheok) — very much
굉장히 (goeng-jang-hi) — extremely
몹시 (mop-si) — awfully
대단히 (dae-dan-hi) — greatly

They overlap a lot, and people mix them freely. New to Korean? Start with 진짜.

Try it: 한국어 진짜 어려워! (han-gu-geo jin-jja eo-ryeo-wo) — Korean is really hard!
