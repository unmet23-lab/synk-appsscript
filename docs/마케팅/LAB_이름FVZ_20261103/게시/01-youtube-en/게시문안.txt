제목: There's no F in Korean… so Zara becomes a turtle 🐢 #learnkorean

설명:
Korean has no F, V or Z sounds, so names change:

Fiona → 피오나 (pi-o-na)
Victor → 빅터 (bik-teo)
Zara → 자라 (ja-ra) — and 자라 is also a soft-shell turtle 🐢
Bob → 밥 (bap) — and 밥 means rice 🍚

Most of the time: F → ㅍ · V → ㅂ · Z → ㅈ
