제목: 3 levels of "sorry" in Korean 🙇 미안 · 미안해요 · 죄송합니다 #learnkorean

설명:
미안 (mi-an) — sorry. Friends only.
미안해요 (mi-an-hae-yo) — I'm sorry. Polite.
죄송합니다 (joe-song-ham-ni-da) — I'm very sorry. For teachers, bosses and anyone older.

전하 (jeon-ha) = Your Majesty. Palace dramas only. Please don't try it on your teacher.
