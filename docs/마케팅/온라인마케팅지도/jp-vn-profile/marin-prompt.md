## 전체 프롬프트

Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: one finished square social-channel profile portrait of Marin (마린), a SYNK mascot.
Input image 1: canonical character identity reference, not an image to redesign.
Create a fresh friendly static portrait on pure neutral white #FFFFFF. Show this exact tactile needle-felt character greeting the viewer: a very slight curious head tilt and one existing short mitten hand lifted in a small, relaxed hello beside the lower edge of its helmet. The pose is restrained and charming, not a military salute. Keep exactly two short arms; do not stretch or add fingers.
Frame the entire rounded navy helmet and upper torso large, centered, with about 10% breathing room above the helmet and enough side room for circular avatar cropping. All of the helmet, both lenses and raised hand must fit comfortably inside the central circular safe area. The lower body can be cropped at the bottom because this is a portrait, not a full-body sprite. Do not draw a circular border.
Preserve the reference's distinctive helmet proportions, deep indigo felt color, subtle stitched vertical seams, broad helmet bottom rim, small stone-beige wool body, original diagonal pocket/sling and single tiny white-and-yellow flower with green stem. Render short individual felt fibers, fine fuzzy edge hairs, real thread and gentle self-shadowing clearly, like a beautifully photographed handmade needle-felt miniature. The body may be beige but the BACKGROUND must be pure white, not cream or yellow.
Marin's two warm yellow eye lenses remain matte felt discs with their same size, spacing and gently oval contour, softly brighter in a welcoming expression. These are NOT biological eyes: no pupils, black eye interiors, eyelids, wink, glossy reflections, neon glow, eyebrows, nose or mouth. Express friendliness through the slight head tilt and small wave only.
Soft neutral studio light, crisp readable silhouette at small avatar sizes, meticulous material detail without gritty noise or excessive shaggy fur. No plastic, metal, anime restyling, new accessories, text, logos, flags, props, other characters or watermark. One square high-resolution PNG suitable as a profile image. This is a new static portrait only, not a replacement for canonical animation sprites.
