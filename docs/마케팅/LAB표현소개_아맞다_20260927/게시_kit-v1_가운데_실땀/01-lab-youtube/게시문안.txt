아 맞다! (a matda) = “Oh, right!” — what Koreans say the moment they suddenly remember something.

아 맞다, 우산! — Oh right, my umbrella!
…아 맞다! 숙제! — …Oh right! Homework!

Pronunciation: 맞다 sounds like [mat-tta]. The ㅈ closes into a short “t”, and 다 becomes a tense 따.
