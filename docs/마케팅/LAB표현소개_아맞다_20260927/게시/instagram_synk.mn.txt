Forgot something? Koreans say 아 맞다! (a matda) = “Oh, right!” 🧶

아 맞다, 우산! — Oh right, my umbrella!
…아 맞다! 숙제! — …Oh right! Homework!

🔊 맞다 sounds like [mat-tta]

What do you always forget? Fill in the blank 👇
