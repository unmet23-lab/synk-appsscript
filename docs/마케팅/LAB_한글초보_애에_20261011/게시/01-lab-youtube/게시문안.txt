Sing along with Mongle — ㅐ and ㅔ look different, but they sound the same: “e”.

Add one stick and the sound changes:
아 → 애 · 어 → 에
가 → 개 · 거 → 게 · 나 → 내 · 너 → 네 · 사 → 새 · 서 → 세

모양만 달라, 둘 다 에! (moyangman dalla, dul da e!) — only the shape is different, both say “e”!
"이건 뭐?" (What is this?) — 개 (dog) and 게 (crab) sound exactly the same; the spelling tells them apart.
Then say hello politely — 안녕하세요 (annyeonghaseyo) — and answer 네! (ne — yes!).

Romanization appears under every letter: ㅐ is written “ae” and ㅔ “e” — different spelling, same sound, just like in Hangul.
English captions (CC) have the lyrics.
At the end, try reading 배 · 카페 · 메뉴 (boat · café · menu).
