아니 = “no”… right? Not always! 🤔
Same word, different tone:
😲 아니?! [a-ni?!] (tone goes up) — “What?!” — when you’re shocked
🎁 아니~ 뭘 이런 걸! [a-ni~ mwol i-reon geol!] (up and down) — “Aww, you shouldn’t have!” — when someone gives you a gift

Your turn: say both out loud! 🎤
