Sing along with Mongle — read your first real Korean words with the Hangul letters you already know.

Read one block at a time, then put the blocks together:
반! 가! 워! 요! → 반가워요 (bangawoyo) — nice to meet you
고! 마! 워! 요! → 고마워요 (gomawoyo) — thank you
잘! 가! 요! → 잘 가요 (jal gayo) — bye, take care
안녕 (annyeong) — hi / bye, to friends · 저는 몽글 — I'm Mongle · 다시 만나요 (dasi mannayo) — see you again

"이건 뭐?" (What is this?) — read the word before the song does!
Every word uses only letters from the first four songs, and each one sounds exactly the way it is written.
Romanization appears under every letter, and English captions (CC) have the lyrics.
At the end, try reading 잘 자요 · 어서 와요 · 파이팅 (good night · welcome · you can do it!).
