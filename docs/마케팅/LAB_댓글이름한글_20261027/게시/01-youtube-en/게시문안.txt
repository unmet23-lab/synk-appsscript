More: Mark → 마크 (ma·keu) · James → 제임스 (je·im·seu)

Korean writes names by how they sound, not by spelling — sounds like the g in "gr-" and the final s get their own block (그 · 스), so a one-syllable name becomes four blocks. (These follow the official Korean spelling for foreign names.)
