설명:
먹지 마 = DON'T eat
In Korean, "don't" comes at the very END of the sentence: verb + 지 마.

케이크 (cake) + 먹지 (eat…) + 마 (…don't!)
= 케이크 먹지 마! → "Don't eat the cake!"

먹지 마 → casual (friends, roommates)
먹지 마세요 → polite

Real handwriting looks different from the textbook, so read slowly — but read it ALL. 😅
