설명:
ㅈ (say "jee-eut") has 2 shapes — both are correct:
1 a line on top, then two legs (like most fonts on your phone)
2 the top line bends down into the left leg, then one more leg (common in Korean handwriting and some fonts)

The only wrong one? A Z.
