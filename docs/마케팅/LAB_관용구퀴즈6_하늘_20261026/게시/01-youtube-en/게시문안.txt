Can you guess these 3 Korean sky & weather idioms in 3 seconds? 🌬️⭐☀️

바람맞다 — not “hit by the wind”: the person you were meeting never showed up (you got stood up)
하늘의 별 따기 — not just “picking a star from the sky”: something super hard to get
해가 서쪽에서 뜨다 — not “the sun rises in the west”: something that never happens… just happened

Say it: 바람맞았어요 · 하늘의 별 따기예요 · 해가 서쪽에서 뜨겠어요
