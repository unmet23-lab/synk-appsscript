설명:
한 시간만요 (han si-gan-man-yo) — "Just one hour, please."

You can say it at a Korean comic café (만화카페, man-hwa ka-pe): rent a tiny room with a blanket, read comics or nap, and pay by the hour.

When your time is up, the staff may ask:
연장하시겠어요? (yeon-jang-ha-si-ge-sseo-yo) — "Would you like to extend?"

To say no:
아니요, 괜찮아요. (a-ni-yo, gwaen-chan-a-yo) — "No, thank you."

Prices and rules differ by café — check the sign at the counter.
