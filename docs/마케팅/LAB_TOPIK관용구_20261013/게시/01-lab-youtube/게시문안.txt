Can you guess these 3 Korean idioms in 3 seconds? 🧶

손이 크다 — not “big hands”: generous (cooks way too much!)
발이 넓다 — not “wide feet”: knows lots of people
눈이 높다 — not “high eyes”: has high standards

Say it: 손이 커요 · 발이 넓어요 · 눈이 높아요
