Can you guess these 3 Korean animal idioms in 3 seconds? 🪰🐸🐱

파리 날리다 — not “flying flies”: a shop with no customers
우물 안 개구리 — not just “a frog in a well”: someone who only knows a tiny world
고양이 세수하듯 — not “washing like a cat”: a quick splash instead of a real wash

Say it: 파리만 날려요 · 우물 안 개구리였어요 · 고양이 세수만 했어요
