제목: 눈 means EYE and SNOW 😂 (snow in my eye = 눈에 눈) #learnkorean

설명:
눈 (nun) = eye 👁 AND snow ❄️
Same word, two things — so when snow lands in your eye, that's 눈에 눈 (nu-ne nun) 😂

Words in this video:
이거, 눈! (i-geo, nun!) = This is 눈 (eye)!
저것도 눈! (jeo-geot-do nun!) = That's 눈 too (snow)!
눈에 눈! (nu-ne nun!) = Snow in my eye!
안 보여~! (an bo-yeo~!) = I can't see~!

Tip: In careful standard Korean, 눈 for "snow" has a long vowel [nuːn], but in everyday speech most people say both the same way.
안 보여 is casual — with someone older, say 안 보여요 (an bo-yeo-yo).
