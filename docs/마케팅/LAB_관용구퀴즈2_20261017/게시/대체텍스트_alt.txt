Felt mascot Mongle hosts a Korean idiom quiz. A felt belly with a popping belly button, paper-thin felt ears and felt lips biting a dumbbell show the literal readings of 배꼽이 빠지다, 귀가 얇다 and 입이 무겁다; a laughing felt face, a tablet with a pile of shopping bags, and zipped lips with a locked box show the real meanings.
