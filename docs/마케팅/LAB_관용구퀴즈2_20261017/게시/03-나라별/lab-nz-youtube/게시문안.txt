Can you guess these 3 Korean idioms in 3 seconds? 😂

배꼽이 빠지다 — not “your belly button falls out”: laughing so hard
귀가 얇다 — not “thin ears”: believes anything you hear
입이 무겁다 — not “a heavy mouth”: keeps secrets

Say it: 배꼽 빠지겠어요 · 귀가 얇아요 · 입이 무거워요
