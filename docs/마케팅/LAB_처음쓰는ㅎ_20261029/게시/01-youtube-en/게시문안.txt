제목: How to write "haha" in Korean 🎩 #learnkorean

설명:
haha → 하하 (ha·ha)

ㅎ = h — draw a little hat (short line, long line), then a face (circle).
ㅏ = a — a tall line with a short tick to the right.
ㅎ + ㅏ = 하, so haha = 하하 😆

Texting tip: Koreans also type ㅎㅎ for a soft "hehe".
