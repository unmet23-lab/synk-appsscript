설명:
ㅍ (say "pee-eup") is written in 4 strokes:
1 top line
2 left line
3 right line
4 bottom line (a little longer)

Keep the two short lines between the top and bottom lines — if they poke out, it turns into #.
Easy way to remember: a little dog standing on the floor. Top = its back, bottom = the floor.
