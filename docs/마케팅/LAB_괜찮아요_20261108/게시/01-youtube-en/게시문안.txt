제목: 괜찮아요 does NOT mean "yes" 😅 #learnkorean

설명:
When someone offers you something in Korean, 괜찮아요 usually means "No, thanks."

괜찮아요 (gwaen-cha-na-yo) = No, thanks (when you're offered something)
네, 좋아요! (ne, jo-a-yo) = Yes, please!
네, 주세요 (ne, ju-se-yo) works too.

괜찮아요 can also mean "I'm okay / it's fine" — but as an answer to "Want a bite?", it's a polite no.
The snack is 붕어빵 (bung-eo-ppang), a fish-shaped pastry Koreans love in autumn and winter 🐟
