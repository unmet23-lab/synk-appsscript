POV: you learned Korean from an app… and the barista asks you to dinner?! 🍚 #learnkorean

[설명]
“다음에 밥 한번 먹어요!” (Let's grab a meal sometime!) — your textbook brain says: a dinner invite! 📅
In real life it's usually just a friendly goodbye, like “see you around.” No date needed. 😅

When you hear it, just say:
👉 네, 좋아요! — Sounds good!
(Between friends you'll hear the casual version: 다음에 밥 한번 먹자!)
(If they really mean it, they'll ask you for a date. 😉)

What other Korean “polite lies” do you know? 👇
