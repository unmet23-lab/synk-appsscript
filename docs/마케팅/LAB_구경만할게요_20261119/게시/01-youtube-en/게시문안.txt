제목: Korean shop staff surround you? Say THIS 😅 #learnkorean

설명:
구경만 할게요 (gu-gyeong-man hal-ge-yo) = I'm just looking 🛍️
Say it when shop staff keep handing you samples — they'll smile and let you browse.
…just don't say 예쁘다 ("so pretty") out loud 😂

Words in this video:
어서 오세요 (eo-seo o-se-yo) = welcome
이거 써 보세요 (i-geo sseo bo-se-yo) = try this one
이것도요 (i-geot-do-yo) = this one too
구경만 할게요 (gu-gyeong-man hal-ge-yo) = I'm just looking
네~ 천천히 보세요 (ne~ cheon-cheon-hi bo-se-yo) = sure, take your time
와… 예쁘다 (wa… ye-ppeu-da) = wow… so pretty
이거요?! (i-geo-yo?!) = this one?!

Tip: 구경 (gu-gyeong) = looking around / sightseeing, 만 (man) = only.
