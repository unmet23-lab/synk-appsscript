제목: How to call a waiter in Korea: 여기요! 🙋 #learnkorean

설명:
In a Korean restaurant, you usually call the server yourself. One word does it:

여기요! (yeo-gi-yo) = Over here, please!
여기 (yeo-gi) = here
요 (yo) = the polite ending

저기요 (jeo-gi-yo) works too, and many tables have a call button. 여기요 only calls the server, so you still order after that 😄
