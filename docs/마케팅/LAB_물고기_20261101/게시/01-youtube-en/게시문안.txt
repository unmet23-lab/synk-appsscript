제목: In Korean, a fish is 'water meat' 🐟 물고기 #learnkorean

설명:
물 (mul) means water. Korean builds a lot of words just by adding them up:

물 + 고기 (meat) = 물고기 — fish
눈 (eye) + 물 = 눈물 — tears
물 + 개 (dog) = 물개 — seal

Say them with me: mul-go-gi, nun-mul, mul-gae.
