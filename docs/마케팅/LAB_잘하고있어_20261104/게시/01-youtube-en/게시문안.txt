설명:
Two Korean lines for the days it all goes wrong:

괜찮아 (gwaen-cha-na) = It's okay.
잘하고 있어 (jal-ha-go i-sseo) = You're doing well.

Both are casual, for friends and family, or for yourself. To someone older, say 괜찮아요 and 잘하고 있어요.

Day 1, you couldn't even write 가 (ga). Now, even your mistakes are whole sentences. That counts.
