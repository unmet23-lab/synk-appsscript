Sing along with Mongle — the Korean final consonants (batchim) ㄴ ㄹ ㅁ ㅇ in one song.

A batchim sits at the bottom of a syllable:
아 + ㄴ = 안 (an) · 아 + ㄹ = 알 (al) · 아 + ㅁ = 암 (am) · 아 + ㅇ = 앙 (ang)
Then read real words: 산 (mountain) · 물 (water) · 밤 (chestnut / night) · 강 (river)
손 (hand) · 눈 (snow) · 별 (star) · 말 (horse) · 곰 (bear) · 몸 (body) · 공 (ball) · 방 (room)
ㅇ on top makes no sound — shh! At the bottom it says "ng".
