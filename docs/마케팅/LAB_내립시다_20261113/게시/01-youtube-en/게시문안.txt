제목: She said "Let's get off." It was NOT my stop 🚇 내립시다 #learnkorean

설명:
내립시다 (nae-rip-si-da) looks like "let's get off" — 내리다 (get off) + the "let's" ending.

But on a packed subway or bus, people shout it to mean "I'm getting off — coming through!" It's not an invitation. Don't follow them out. 😅

What you can say when it's your stop:
잠시만요, 내릴게요. (jam-si-man-yo, nae-ril-ge-yo) — Excuse me, I'm getting off.

Shorter: 내릴게요! (nae-ril-ge-yo) — Getting off!
