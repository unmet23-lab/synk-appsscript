제목: ㅋㅋㅋ = Korean “lol” 😂 …but just ONE ㅋ? Cold. #learnkorean

설명:
ㅋ (keu) sounds like a little laugh — so Koreans text ㅋㅋㅋ for “lol” 😂
The more ㅋ, the harder you’re laughing:
ㅋㅋ = 🙂
ㅋㅋㅋㅋ = 😄
ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ = 🤣

But a single ㅋ can feel cold or sarcastic 😐 — send at least two!

Words in this video:
ㅋㅋㅋ (keu-keu-keu) = lol (laughing in texts)
…ㅋ? (keu?) = just one… cold
