제목: Korean slang: 'I want to FREEZE-DRY my mom' 😳 #learnkorean

설명:
엄마 동결건조하고 싶다 (eom-ma dong-gyeol-geon-jo-ha-go sip-da)
Literally: "I want to freeze-dry my mom" 😳
Real meaning: "I wish Mom could stay just as she is, forever" 💗

Freeze-dried food keeps its shape for years — so in Korean internet slang, "freeze-dry" someone = keep them exactly as they are now. Mongle took it literally 😂

Words in this video:
동결건조 (dong-gyeol-geon-jo) = freeze-dried
동결… 건조? (dong-gyeol… geon-jo?) = freeze… dry?!
엄마를… 말린다고?! (eom-ma-reul… mal-lin-da-go?!) = you'd DRY your mom?!
간식 챙겨 가~ (gan-sik chaeng-gyeo ga~) = take your snacks~
봐, 동결건조 딸기. 그대로지? (bwa, dong-gyeol-geon-jo ttal-gi. geu-dae-ro-ji?) = see? freeze-dried strawberries — same shape!
잠깐… (jam-kkan…) = wait…
엄마, 늙지 마… (eom-ma, neuk-ji ma…) = Mom, don't get old…
얘는 참… (yae-neun cham…) = oh, you…
이렇게? (i-reo-ke?) = like this?
엄마~! (eom-ma~!) = Mom~!
엄마, 동결건조하고 싶어! (eom-ma, dong-gyeol-geon-jo-ha-go si-peo!) = Mom, I want to freeze-dry you!

Tip: it's recent social-media slang, not a dictionary word — Koreans use it with a smile about someone they love.
