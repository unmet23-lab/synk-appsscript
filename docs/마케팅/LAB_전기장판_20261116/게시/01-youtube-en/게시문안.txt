제목: Korean winter survival item: 전기장판 🥶→🫠 #learnkorean

설명:
전기장판 (jeon-gi-jang-pan) = electric heating mat 🔥
Word by word: 전기 (jeon-gi) = electricity + 장판 (jang-pan) = the vinyl floor covering of a Korean room.
Korean homes heat the floor, not the air — and a 전기장판 is a little heated floor you can sleep on.

Words in this video:
추워 (chu-wo) = I’m cold
따뜻해 (tta-tteu-tae) = it’s warm
못 일어나 (mo-di-reo-na) = I can’t get up
포기 (po-gi) = I give up
