Sing along with Mongle — one song, all 14 basic Korean consonants (Hangul).

Every line builds a syllable: consonant + ㅏ (a)
ㄱ + ㅏ = 가 (ga) · ㄴ + ㅏ = 나 (na) · ㄷ + ㅏ = 다 (da) · ㄹ + ㅏ = 라 (ra) …
ㅇ is silent at the start, so ㅇ + ㅏ = 아 (a) — shh!
Consonants are coral, vowels are blue — watch them join into one syllable.

"그 더하기 아는?" (g + a = ?) — say the answer before the song does!
Romanization appears under every letter, and English captions (CC) have the lyrics.
At the end, try reading 나무 · 바다 · 모자 (tree · sea · hat).
