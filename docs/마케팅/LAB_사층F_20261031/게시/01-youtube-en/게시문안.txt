제목: Why Korean elevators have no 4 👻 사 = 4 #learnkorean

설명:
In Korean, 4 is 사 (sa) — and 사 (死) also means death. Same sound.
So many buildings in Korea label the 4th floor F, for Four.

Count with me:
일 (il) 1
이 (i) 2
삼 (sam) 3
사 (sa) 4

Not every building does this, but you'll see it a lot.
