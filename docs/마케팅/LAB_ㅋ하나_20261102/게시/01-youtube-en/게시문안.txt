제목: ㅋㅋㅋ = LOL… but ONE ㅋ = 'k.' 🥶 #learnkorean

설명:
ㅋ is the Korean 'k' sound (keu). Type it again and again and it turns into a laugh:

ㅋㅋ = haha
ㅋㅋㅋ = LOL
ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ = LMAO
ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ… = I'm dead

But just ONE ㅋ reads cold — like texting "k." to a friend. Send at least two!
