First time writing ㄹ (rieul)? If yours turned into a 2, a Z or a snake 🐍, you're not alone.

The fix: ㄹ = ㄱ + a line + ㄴ. Three strokes, top to bottom.
1. ㄱ: across, then down
2. a short line across the middle, left to right
3. ㄴ: down, then across (make the bottom line a little longer)
Lift your pencil between strokes. Fast handwriting joins them later. Start with three.
