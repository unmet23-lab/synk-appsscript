Sing and move along with Mongle — Korean double vowels in one song.

Two vowels glide into one:
오 + 아 = 와 (wa) · 우 + 어 = 워 (wo) · 우 + 이 = 위 (wi) · 으 + 이 = 의 (ui)
With a consonant: 과 (gwa, as in 사과 apple) · 귀 (gwi, ear) · 쥐 (jwi, mouse) · 뭐 (mwo, what?)
The first vowel is coral, the second is blue — watch them join.

"오 더하기 아는?" (o + a = ?) — say the answer before the song does!
Romanization appears under every letter, and English captions (CC) have the lyrics.
At the end, try reading 봐 · 줘 · 쉬 before you hear them!
