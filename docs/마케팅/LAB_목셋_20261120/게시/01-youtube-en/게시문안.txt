제목: Korean has 3 'necks'?! 🧣 #learnkorean

설명:
손목 (son-mok) = wrist = 손 hand + 목 neck 🧣
발목 (bal-mok) = ankle = 발 foot + 목 neck
So in Korean you have 3 "necks" — and Mongle has none 😂

Words in this video:
목 (mok) = neck
손목 (son-mok) = wrist
발목 (bal-mok) = ankle
으, 추워! (eu, chu-wo!) = brr, so cold!
목이… 셋?! (mo-gi… set?!) = three… necks?!
너도! (neo-do!) = you too!
내 목은…? (nae mo-geun…?) = where's my neck…?
따뜻해~ (tta-tteu-tae~) = so warm~

Tip: 목 is the narrow part that joins two bigger parts — your wrist joins hand and arm, your ankle joins foot and leg.
