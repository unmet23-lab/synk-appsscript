제목: No English word for 아까워 😭 (too good to waste) #learnkorean

설명:
아까워 (a-kka-wo) = "too good to waste" 😭
English has no single word for it — and Mongle will NOT let that last spoonful go.

You can say 아까워 when:
🍚 food is left on the plate
⏰ your time gets wasted
💸 money goes on something useless

Words in this video:
다 드셨죠~? (da deu-syeot-jyo~?) = All done? (polite)
아까워! (a-kka-wo!) = Too good to waste!
더 줄게~ (deo jul-ge~) = I'll give you more~

Tip: 아까워 is casual. With someone older or a stranger, say 아까워요 (a-kka-wo-yo).
