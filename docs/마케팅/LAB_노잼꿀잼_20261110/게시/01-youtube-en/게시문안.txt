제목: Koreans say "no jam" when it's boring 🍓 노잼 vs 꿀잼 #learnkorean

설명:
잼 (jaem) here is NOT jam. It's short for 재미 (jae-mi) = fun.

노잼 (no-jaem) = no + fun → boring
꿀잼 (kkul-jaem) = honey + fun → super fun (꿀 = honey)

Try it:
이 영화 노잼이야. (i yeong-hwa no-jaem-i-ya) — This movie is boring.
이 영화 꿀잼이야! (i yeong-hwa kkul-jaem-i-ya) — This movie is so fun!

Both are casual slang for friends — not for your boss or professor.
