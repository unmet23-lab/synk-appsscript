  • 성장 리포트 카드는 보호자 이메일로 이미지 파일을 첨부해 보내드립니다.
  • 녹음 파일은 외부에 공유하지 않습니다. 대신 학생 앱의 「목소리 타임랩스」에서 처음과 지금의 발음을 글로 옮긴 기록으로 성장을 확인하실 수 있습니다.
  • 그 밖의 학생 정보(연락처·상담 내용·성적 원본)도 학원 계정으로 접근이 제한되어 있습니다.

보호자 이메일이 등록되어 있지 않으면 리포트 카드를 보내드릴 수 없습니다 — 상담 시 이메일을 꼭 알려주세요.

Privacy Policy

Data controller: SYNK (business registration 697-65-00332), 23-2 Usadan-ro 2ga-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea — operating the Korean-language academy SYNK LAB in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia.
Services covered: the automated consultation assistant on our Facebook Page "SYNK LAB", and the SYNK LAB learning app.

1 Information we collect

  • (a) Enquiry / enrolment form. Student name, date of birth, contact details; guardian name, phone and email; learning background and goals; interests; free-text answers (difficulties with Korean, why they chose us, 3–5 year goals).
  • (b) Facebook Messenger assistant. The full content of messages exchanged, the page-scoped user ID Facebook assigns to the conversation, and any name, contact details or child's age you volunteer when requesting a consultation. We do not request contact details from users who appear to be minors; we ask them to have a guardian contact us instead.
  • (c) App usage by enrolled students. Attendance, homework and its content, quiz answers, learning points and progress records, Korean practice conversations with our AI, and an internal "drop-out risk" rating (see 2).
  • (d) Voice recordings — required for our speaking course. Audio recorded during lessons, pronunciation practice or mock interviews, and its transcript. This is not optional for that course: our speaking method teaches from the student's own voice, so without consent we cannot provide pronunciation correction, mock interviews or the conversation app. Other courses remain available.
  • (e) Photos, video and project outputs — optional. Images and video recorded during lessons and K-culture activities (including face, voice and the full footage), and class project outputs. Nothing is published without consent: use for academy promotion (social media, website, brochures) is a separate opt-in choice on the enrolment card, and consent may be withdrawn at any time. Declining changes nothing about the service — lessons, the app and progress records are provided identically. Unlike (d), this is not a condition of enrolment.

2 How we use it

Consultation and class placement; running lessons and tracking attendance and progress; personalising learning content; sending weekly progress reports to guardians in Mongolian; assessing drop-out risk so we can reach out and help — this rating is not an academic evaluation and is never used to disadvantage a student; aggregate operational statistics; and developing and improving Korean-learning services (apps and AI learning tools) built by SYNK LAB and its affiliated or successor entities. When data is exported for that development purpose, names and contact details are removed. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

3 Automated processing

Consultations on our Facebook Page are first answered by an automated bot, which discloses that it is automated at the start of every conversation. It does not answer questions outside its approved knowledge base (for example tuition fees, which are not finalised); those are passed to a human teacher. You may ask to speak to a person at any time. The drop-out risk rating is calculated automatically but no decision about a student is made on that rating alone — a person reviews it.

4 Processors

Google (Workspace, Sheets, Drive, Forms, Gmail, Apps Script) for storage, form collection and email; Google Cloud Speech-to-Text for transcription — the audio file itself is sent; Glide for the student/guardian app; Anthropic (Claude AI) to generate replies, homework feedback and learning feedback — message and practice text is sent; and Meta (Facebook/Messenger) as the consultation channel. These providers' servers are located outside Mongolia (primarily in the United States).

5 Retention

Voice recordings and transcripts, and enrolled students' learning and progress records: retained without a fixed time limit, to support multi-year progress records. Consultation conversations and contact details (including from people who do not enrol): kept for consultation history and deleted on request. System backups: 30 days, plus up to 30 further days in trash before permanent deletion.

6 How guardians receive their child's material

Student voice recordings and progress report cards are not published on the internet — they can only be opened by an authenticated academy account. Report cards are delivered to the guardian's email as an image attachment. Recordings are not shared externally; instead the student app shows a "voice timelapse" comparing transcripts of their first and most recent recordings. All other student data (contact details, consultation content, source records) is likewise restricted to academy accounts.

7 Your rights — access, correction, deletion, withdrawal

Contact us using the details in section 11 to request access, correction, deletion (of recordings, learning records and consultation conversations), or withdrawal of consent. On withdrawal we stop using the data immediately and delete stored recordings. One honest limitation: material already used to train an AI model cannot be reversed, because there is no way to remove one person's voice from a model after training; we do not use it after withdrawal. We respond within 7 days; backup copies are erased on the cycle in section 5.

8 Minors

Personal data of minors is collected on the basis of guardian consent. Our assistant does not request contact details from users who appear to be minors. Guardians may exercise all rights in section 7 on their child's behalf.

9 Security

Data is held in storage restricted to academy accounts. Access is granted only to staff who need it. Text received from outside is handled so that it cannot execute as a command in our systems.
