HOW IT SOUNDS

Written 맞다,
said [맏따]

아 맏 따

a · mat · tta

The ㅈ at the end turns into a short stop, and 다 gets a tense “tt”. Say it fast, like a little gasp.

USE IT TODAY

Three things
people always forget

  • 아 맞다, 숙제!
    Oh right — my homework!
  • 아 맞다, 열쇠!
    Oh right — my keys!
  • 아 맞다, 오늘 생일이지!
    Oh right — it’s your birthday today!
