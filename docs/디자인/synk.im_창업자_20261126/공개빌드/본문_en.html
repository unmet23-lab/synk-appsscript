    • 1 minEvery minute, it logs the health of our computers and servers.

    Staff app · SYNK Workspace

    Clock in, and the work
    comes card by card

    Messages, to-dos, post approvals and client cuts all land on one screen. Whatever a client is waiting on comes first, and when one card is done, the next one moves up.

    We built the app ourselves, with no outsourcing. When we spot something slowing the work down, we fix it on the spot.
