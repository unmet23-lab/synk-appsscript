THE CAFE (the same room in every image of this set): a small student-run café on the ground floor of a Korean university's student-union building, right next door to the student cafeteria — run by a student start-up club. One cozy room. Fixed features that must always look the same:
- A long service counter: light natural-oak countertop over a front of small square WHITE tiles. On it: a brushed-steel two-group espresso machine with no logo, a small glass pastry case with a few croissants and cookies, a stack of plain white mugs, a small brass service bell.
- Behind the counter: open light-oak shelves with glass jars of coffee beans and plain tea tins (no labels), and one trailing pothos plant.
- Three warm amber glass-globe pendant lights hang in a row over the counter.
- Tall windows along one wall look out onto a campus walkway with bare trees.
- Seating: small round tables and mismatched second-hand wooden chairs, plus a long window bar with tall stools.
- Staff aprons are navy canvas with cream straps (the university's navy-and-cream colors).
- Walls are warm cream; floor is light oak planks.
