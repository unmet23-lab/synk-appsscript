THE STUDENT — a Mongolian young woman of about 19, a first-year international student from Erdenet; an original person. First the everyday Mongolian features: a round, full face with broad, high cheekbones and naturally deep-rosy, wind-kissed cheeks; light warm skin with a few faint freckle-like spots across the nose; wide-set monolid dark-brown eyes with straight lashes; soft, straight brows; a small nose with a low bridge; full lips. Then what makes her lovely: a sunny, open face and a warm laugh — cute and memorable rather than model-like. Thick straight black hair in a long low braid over one shoulder, a few loose strands at the temples. She wears a mustard-yellow crew-neck sweatshirt. Small pearl stud earrings; no glasses.
