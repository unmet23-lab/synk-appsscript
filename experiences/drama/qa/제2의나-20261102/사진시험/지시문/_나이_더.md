THE FACE OF A 19-YEAR-OLD (they read as 30 or older in Image 1): fuller, softer cheeks, a slender jaw without jowls or a double chin, no temple recession — a full, youthful hairline and thick hair with no grey, no age spots, clean-shaven. Keep their own face width and jaw shape — only the sagging and jowls are gone, the jaw is not slimmed. Keep their eye shape, brows, nose and lips recognisably theirs, so it reads as the same person at nineteen.
