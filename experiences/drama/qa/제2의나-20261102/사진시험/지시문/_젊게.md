ALWAYS, WHATEVER THE LOOK: this story is about a first-year university student, so show them as they would look at about 19 to 20 years old — whatever their age in Image 1 (a teenager looks a little older, someone in their 30s, 40s or 50s looks much younger), while staying clearly the same person: the same face shape, eyes, brows, nose and lips, only younger or older. No wrinkles, no forehead lines, no creases around the eyes or mouth, no under-eye bags or dark circles, no sagging; firm, smooth, youthful skin with natural elasticity and a healthy glow. Keep a fine natural skin texture so it never looks airbrushed, waxy or plastic. Facial hair: clean-shaven unless the student chose to keep it. When they smile, the forehead stays relaxed and smooth and the brows stay relaxed, never raised high.
