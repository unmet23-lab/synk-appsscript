PLACE: a university dance practice room on a high floor in Seoul, one week before the campus festival — the room where a student who dreams of the stage practises after class. A long wall mirror runs along the left wall, seen at a steep angle so it only reflects the room's lights. Light maple sprung wooden floor with a soft sheen and a few strips of white floor tape. The main ceiling lights are off: a thin LED strip along the bottom of the mirror glows warm coral, a second strip under the ceiling glows soft blue, and one warm floor lamp stands in the corner. Lived-in details only at the edges: a small portable speaker on the floor with a tiny lit power dot, a clear water bottle, a folded towel, white sneakers, a hoodie over a folding chair, a smartphone on a small tripod.
HERO, FOREGROUND: slightly left of centre and close to the camera, a silver wireless handheld microphone stands upright in the clip of a black mic stand; it is sharp and catches a coral rim light from the LED strip. The microphone head sits at about 55% of the frame height; the stand runs down out of the bottom edge.
COMPOSITION: the top 45% of the frame, behind the microphone, is calm and softly out of focus (window, city bokeh and mirror) — a large title will be placed there later. Camera at standing chest height, 35mm lens, f/2.8.
