THE VISITOR: a Korean man, about 47, the friendly owner of the fried-chicken shop near the campus, a local regular who comes to every festival. Short neat black-and-grey hair, thinning a little at the temples; a warm, lived-in, slightly rounder face with clear crow's feet and laugh lines; playful narrowed eyes; a short tidy stubble with grey in it; a slight dad belly. A faded navy short-sleeve work shirt, a brown canvas crossbody bag.
He is chatting in an easygoing, cheeky way, as if hoping to talk his way in — one eyebrow a little raised, a sly half-smile — talking to the staff member in the window, lips parted mid-word, looking straight into the camera.
